# November source audit

Reviewed upstream c5d93b9 against FluvalConnect 1.0.15, version code 110,
APK SHA-256 9c3cecf8edebff06ac945d7431b078813af9d4fd86ba9fba40453dc612be3415.

## Repairs

- Channel transitions now schedule steps against monotonic deadlines across
  the requested duration, including durations shorter than a step interval.
- Channel failures preserve reconnect state, per-channel notification revisions,
  and successful intermediate power/mode operations. An equal-valued report
  still counts as fresh readback. Failed Off after successful zero-channel
  application does not restore the preceding nonzero channels.
- FACEBD daylight-saving commands commit their requested value only after a
  successful write without newer readback. Failure never restores an old cache.

## Source comparison

- Classic commands: OldLightKxtKt defines 6802 mode, 6803 power, 6804 channels,
  6805 read, 6806 preset, 680A weather, 680E clock and 680F identify. Percentage
  channel writes use little-endian words scaled by ten and XOR checksum.
- Current controllers: LightKxtKt defines FACEBD keys 99 and 101–123 and
  FFF0/SPP D0 reads, D1 commands and D2 status. Product identity and live GATT
  transport remain separate concerns.
- LightDeviceUtils supplies channel counts, old/current classification,
  spectrum selection and zero/four/eleven-effect capabilities. DeviceUtil
  supplies the older product-name/channel exceptions. Product 281 deliberately
  retains the APK's default spectrum route and its separate channel labels.
- ManFragment selects the six spectrum assets. Their checksums match the
  recorded APK colour evidence. Arbitrary RGB fitting is integration code;
  the app itself presents independent emitter sliders.
- TimeUtil uses Monday=1 through Sunday=7. Classic and current clock builders
  preserve their separate formats. FACEBD DST is independent of clock sync.
- AutoFragment and ProFragment calculate preview curves with integer levels,
  midnight wrapping and stable time ordering. Integration schedule reporting
  is explicitly assumed output; it is not instantaneous illumination readback.

## Home Assistant review

Reviewed config-entry discovery and options reload, pending entity creation,
entity subscriptions/timers, unload cleanup, device-registry cleanup, shared
actions, diagnostic redaction and retained schedule-readback API. The changes
do not alter command bytes, product capabilities, minimum HA version, entity
identifiers or fixture schedules.

## Validation boundaries

The regression suite exercises transport decoding, malformed frames, channel
widths, command failures, lifecycle cleanup and schedule boundaries. This audit
does not execute the Android application or fixture firmware. It adds no new
physical validation for FACEBD/SPP fixtures and does not prove optical colour
accuracy or instantaneous scheduled illumination. No live fixture was operated.
